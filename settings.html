How Habit Dashboard Works

Track one day at a time.

Submit one entry per day with your habits, ratings, sleep, time use, and notes.

Look for weekly patterns.

The dashboard becomes more useful after several days of data, especially after about 7 days. Charts show longer-term patterns over time.

Compare completed weeks.

Weekly Summary compares the last 7 completed days against the previous 7 completed days.

Use context with the numbers.

Daily Notes explain what was happening behind the numbers, while Raw Data lets you inspect the entries underneath.

Choose what you track.

Habit choices control what is visible and available in the app. They can be edited later and do not change the database structure.

About Overall Score.

Overall Score is entered by you. It is not calculated from Physical, Mental, and Energy scores.
